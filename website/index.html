Installation & Permissions

How to Install Soundwave on Android

Because Soundwave is 100% free and open source, it is distributed directly as an APK file. Follow these 3 easy steps to install.

1

Download APK

Tap the Download APK button on this page. The official verified file Soundwave.apk (approx. 12 MB) will download to your device.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

When opening the downloaded APK, if Android displays a prompt, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source" (Chrome / Files).

3

Install & Stream

Tap Install. Once complete, open Soundwave, select your favorite languages, and enjoy unlimited ad-free music in studio quality!

Why does Android ask for "Install unknown apps" permission?

Android labels any app downloaded outside the Google Play Store as an "unknown source". Soundwave is 100% safe, verified, open-source, and does not collect personal tracking data. You can inspect the entire codebase on our GitHub repository.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Soundwave, compatibility, audio quality, and updates.

Is Soundwave really 100% free with no ads?
Yes! Soundwave is built as an open-source community music client. There are no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no audio ads, and no paywalls. Everything is unlocked for free.
How does the 320kbps HD audio engine work?
Soundwave connects directly to lossless 320kbps CD-quality music streams from official music catalogs. You get pure uncompressed audio with full dynamic range and punchy bass.
How do I receive app updates?
Soundwave features an automatic built-in OTA update system. Whenever a new update is released, the app will notify you with a changelog and let you update seamlessly with one tap.
What Android versions are supported?
Soundwave is fully compatible with Android 7.0 (Nougat) all the way up to Android 14 and Android 15+.
Can I listen to downloaded music offline?
Yes. You can download any track with a single tap to your device storage and listen offline without an active internet connection.
